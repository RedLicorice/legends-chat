# Legends Chat — User Manual

Welcome to **Legends Chat**, a private community chat platform built for real conversation. This guide covers everything you need to get started and make the most of your experience.

---

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

There are two ways to join:

- **Register directly** — go to the registration page and sign up with your email address and a password.
- **Use an invite code** — if someone has shared an invite link or code with you, follow it to create your account.

Once registered, you'll land in the main chat interface and can start exploring channels right away.

---

## Signing In

Legends Chat supports three login methods. You can use any combination of them depending on what you've set up in Settings.

- **Passkey** — the fastest and most secure option. Passkeys are phishing-resistant and tied to your device. Recommended.
- **Email + Password** — classic login with your registered email and password.
- **Telegram magic link** — authenticate via the Legends Chat Telegram bot. The bot sends you a one-time link; tap it to log in.

### Two-Factor Authentication

For extra security on email accounts, you can enable **TOTP 2FA** (time-based one-time passwords, compatible with apps like Google Authenticator or Authy). Enable it in **Settings → Security**.

You can also register a **passkey** after your initial login via **Settings → Security**.

## Channels & Navigation

### Finding Your Channels

The **left sidebar** lists all channels you have access to. Each channel shows an **unread message count** so you always know where activity is happening.

- Click any channel name to open it.
- On mobile, tap the **hamburger menu** (top-left) to open the sidebar.

### Sidebar Display Modes

You can customize how the sidebar looks in **Settings → Appearance**:

- **Minimal** — icons only, maximizes your chat area.
- **Strip** — a slightly wider view with icons and labels.

### Channel Types

Not all channels work the same way:

- **Regular channels** — standard group chat. Messages are stored on the server.
- **Feed channels** — bulletin-board style. Posts are more like announcements or articles. Use **Ctrl+Enter** to submit a post.
- **E2EE channels** — end-to-end encrypted. A lock icon is shown next to the channel name. Only participants can read the messages.
- **P2P channels** — direct peer-to-peer. Messages are **not stored on the server** and exist only during your session.

## Sending Messages

Type in the input bar at the bottom of any channel and press **Enter** to send.

- **Shift+Enter** — insert a new line without sending.
- **Ctrl+Enter** — send in feed channels (where Enter adds a new line by default).

### Attachments & Media

- **Paperclip icon** — attach files.
- **Image icon** — attach images.
- **GIF picker** — search and insert GIFs (available if enabled by your community admin).
- **Emoji reactions** — hover over any message and click the smile icon, or long-press on mobile.

### Mentioning Someone

Type **@** followed by a name to mention a user. A popup will appear — select the person you want to tag. They'll receive a notification.

### Hashtags

Type a **#hashtag** anywhere in your message and it will be highlighted automatically.

## Message Formatting

Legends Chat supports Markdown formatting in messages.

| Style | Syntax | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Bold | `*bold*` | **bold** |
| Italic | `_italic_` | _italic_ |
| Inline code | `` `code` `` | `code` |
| Code block | ```` ```code``` ```` | block of code |
| Strikethrough | `~~text~~` | ~~text~~ |
| Blockquote | `> text` | indented quote |
| Link | `[link text](url)` | clickable link |

## Message Actions

### On Desktop

Hover over a message to reveal action icons, or right-click for the full context menu.

### On Mobile

Long-press a message to open the action menu.

### Available Actions

- **Reply** — reply to a specific message; a quote preview is shown in your reply.
- **React** — add an emoji reaction.
- **Copy text** — copy the message content to your clipboard.
- **Edit** — edit your own messages.
- **Delete** — delete your own messages.
- **Report** — flag a message for moderator review.
- **Select** — select multiple messages for bulk actions.

### Threads

When a message has **3 or more replies**, a **"View thread"** button appears. Click it to open the thread in a panel on the right side of the screen.

---

## Polls

Moderators and admins can create polls inside channels. When a poll is posted:

- Click any option to cast your vote.
- Results update in real-time as other members vote.

---

## Notifications

### In-App Notifications

Click the **bell icon** in the sidebar header to view your notification feed.

You are notified when:

- Someone **@mentions** you.
- Someone **replies** to one of your messages.
- An admin sends a **broadcast announcement**.

### Push Notifications

To receive notifications even when the app isn't open:

1. When prompted, click **"Enable notifications"** in the app.
2. Allow notifications in your browser when the browser permission dialog appears.

If you dismissed the prompt, you can re-enable push notifications through your browser's site settings.

## Your Profile & Settings

### Editing Your Profile

Click your **avatar or display name** in the sidebar footer to open your profile. From there you can:

- Change your **display name**.
- Upload or change your **avatar**.
- Set a **profile banner** image.

### Settings Page

Access **Settings** from the sidebar or profile menu. Key options include:

- **Appearance** — choose your theme and sidebar layout (minimal or strip).
- **Security** — enable TOTP 2FA, manage passkeys, and link your email.
- **Notifications** — manage push notification preferences.

### Themes

Legends Chat comes with several themes including dark, cyberpunk, legends, and others your admin may have configured. Go to **Settings → Appearance → Theme** and select the one you like.

## Installing as an App

Legends Chat is a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it on your device for a native app-like experience — no app store required.

- **iOS (Safari)** — tap the Share button, then select **"Add to Home Screen"**.
- **Android (Chrome)** — open the browser menu and tap **"Install App"**.
- **Desktop (Chrome / Edge)** — look for the **install icon** in the address bar and click it.

Once installed, Legends Chat opens in its own window without browser navigation bars.
