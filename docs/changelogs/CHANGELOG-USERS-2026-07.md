# What's New in Legends

*A roundup of everything that's landed recently — private messages, stronger
encryption, a faster mobile app, and lots of polish.*

---

## 💬 Direct Messages
- **Send private 1:1 messages** to anyone, with a **requests inbox** so you
  choose who you talk to — accept or decline.
- **Message bots directly** where they allow it (marked with a **BOT** badge).
- **Optional end-to-end encrypted DMs**, with a lock indicator and a
  **safety-number check** so you can verify you're really talking to the right
  person.
- **One unified chat list** with **All / Topics / DMs / Bots** tabs and a **＋
  button** to start something new.
- **Delete a conversation** — just for you, or for both people — from the chat's
  **⋯** menu. Hidden chats quietly reappear if the other person messages you
  again.

## 🔒 Encryption
- **Channels can now be end-to-end encrypted.** Keys are shared securely with
  each member.
- Encrypted channels always show a **banner naming the moderators who can read
  messages** — encryption keeps out the public internet, not your own
  community's admins. We'd rather be upfront about that.
- **Join-forward history:** when you join an encrypted channel you see messages
  from that point on, not older history.
- You can now **edit your messages in encrypted channels** (this used to do
  nothing).
- Messages you can't read yet show a **tidy blurred placeholder** with a lock
  button explaining exactly why (still loading, key hasn't arrived, you joined
  too late…), and they **unlock automatically** once the key arrives. You can't
  accidentally react or reply to something you can't read.
- **Bots can take part in encrypted chats** too, when enabled.

## 📱 Mobile experience
- **New drill-down navigation:** tap through **List → Chat → Thread** as
  full-screen pages that slide in, with a **Back button** — no more cramped
  split view or fiddly drawer.
- **Bigger, easier tap targets** (switches instead of tiny checkboxes) and
  better spacing around the phone's home-bar area.
- Quick **filter pills** to jump between Topics, DMs, and Bots.

## ⚡ Speed & app feel
- The installed app now **feels native**: the sidebar and layout no longer
  flash or "reload" when you move between chats, admin, or settings.
- The **loading splash shows once** on a cold open, not on every screen.
- **Realtime updates keep flowing** (new messages, DM requests) even while
  you're on Settings or Admin.
- **Revisiting a chat is instant**, and switching channels is roughly **twice
  as fast**.

## 🔔 Notifications
- Tapping a chat notification now **opens the exact message** it's about —
  scrolled to and highlighted — instead of a "not found" page.

## 🔑 Signing in
- **Stay signed in:** the installed app no longer makes you log in again every
  time you reopen it.
- Smoother passkey sign-in (options load ahead of time; clear loading
  spinners).
- If you open the app inside another app's built-in browser, we now **prompt
  you to open it in your real browser** so passkeys and notifications work.
- **@mentions work reliably** even after someone changes their Telegram
  username.

## ✍️ Writing messages
- **Desktop right-click menu:** right-click a message for a compact menu right
  at your cursor; right-click a link to **Copy** or **Open** it (external links
  still pass through the safety warning).
- **Drag a Markdown file** into a channel to drop its contents straight into the
  message box, ready to edit and send.
- **Export your draft** as a Markdown file (feed channels).

## 🛠️ For admins & moderators
- **Create users and hand out one-time sign-in links** without needing Telegram
  first.
- **Bulk-delete** topics and bots; a cleaner master/detail bots screen.
- **Search + paged** users and bots lists (no more silent 100-user cap).
- Tidier, more consistent admin panels; registration/security settings grouped
  sensibly.
